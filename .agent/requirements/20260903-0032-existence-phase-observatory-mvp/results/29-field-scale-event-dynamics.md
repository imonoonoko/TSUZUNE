# Result 29 — field-scale event dynamics

status: isolated R5 mechanically and visually verified; owner aesthetic acceptance pending

## What changed

The prior system continually changed neighbourhoods, but its perceptual vocabulary stayed nearly uniform: drift, curl, and diffuse memory. The new system introduces three concurrent, temporary events. Each event chooses seven carrier notes from a changing epoch, computes its nucleus from their live toroidal positions, and progresses through capture, compression, rupture, afterglow, and dormancy. It therefore has neither a permanent center nor a permanently assigned group.

Nearby note particles are pulled inward, compressed with a small orbit, expelled, and then allowed to circulate through fading residue. Participation also raises temporary event energy, shifts depth, enlarges and cools live light, and deposits more strongly into the same finite decaying history texture. No event label or explicit halo is drawn; the event is visible through the particles' actual redistribution and their residue.

## Theory and product boundary

The event system is an authored local observation vocabulary, not an ontology or natural-science simulation. Capture does not prove relation, compression does not define identity, brightness does not indicate value, and a visible cluster is not a true group. Content, time, explicit links, structure, history, boundary, and re-binding remain fallible influences rather than exhaustive knowledge coordinates.

Exactly 577 current observed notes remain exactly 577 live particles. No decorative particle population, raw title, body, path, fixed graph edge, new dependency, or additional control was added.
