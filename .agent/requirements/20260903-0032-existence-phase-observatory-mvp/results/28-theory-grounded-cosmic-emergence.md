## Theory boundary and design translation

The production TSUZUNE theory notes were read before the redesign. Their central boundary is preserved: `存在相そのもの` precedes and exceeds any finite local description. Particle, force, field, boundary, path, network, terrain, and scalar are possible observation descriptions, not the ontology itself. The working sequence `濃淡 → 流れ → 分化 → 統合 → 安定 → 地形 → 次の流れ` remains an exploratory order rather than a law.

The implementation therefore keeps `content / time / links / structure` as four independent, fallible observation inputs and adds four states that are produced during observation instead of assigned permanently:

- shared, decaying passage memory: prior movement slightly bends later movement and can be erased;
- overlapping, moving observation boundaries: permeability varies by time, scale, and local encounter;
- temporary re-binding stability: gathering can persist briefly, then loosen and separate;
- dynamic depth projection: foreground/background position changes with history and interaction, never note rank.

Proximity remains explicitly insufficient to establish a true relation or group. A stable anonymous seed is used only to break visual symmetry, not to encode meaning or value.
