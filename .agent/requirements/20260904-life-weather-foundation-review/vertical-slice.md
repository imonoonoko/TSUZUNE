# Vertical slice — Living Archive Field

## Purpose

Falsify or support one proposition within one bounded implementation campaign:

> When note identity is preserved as traceable contribution rather than constant visible dots, TSUZUNE can form a continuous, deep, materially changing artwork that still could not have come from another Vault.

This is an isolated art proof. It does not change product source, package dependencies, production installation, or Git delivery.

Field, flow, depth, particles, voxels, and material are local observational representations used by this proof. They are not `存在相そのもの`, a note's value, or a claim about the archive's true relationships.

The implementation is timeboxed to five working days; the twelve-minute viewing and next-day revisit complete the evidence campaign within roughly one week. If the causal and continuity invariants cannot be implemented inside that boundary, stop rather than broadening the engine.

## One visual target

A silent full-frame work entered mid-flow. A few lights can be seen, but the dominant image is a deep field with filaments, thin membranes, voids, and fading wakes. The viewer can follow energy passing through a structure; the camera moves through the same world without a cut when an encounter changes. Nothing resembles a node graph, an atom model, a swarm of insects, or a generic star screensaver.

## Minimum build

1. **Contributor ledger**
   - Preserve all 599 logical note IDs and the existing privacy-safe inputs.
   - Map each note to bounded contributions to density, velocity, boundary response, persistence, and event eligibility.
   - Before any carrier/noise pass, deterministically record every macro event/material region as `{snapshotDigest, seed, archiveTime, regionOrCueId, channel, noteId, normalizedWeight, carrierTerm}`.
   - Declare the source-weight normalization. Record noise, interpolation, and decay as source-free carrier terms; they may texture or transport a source-selected event, but may not choose its cue, primary region, lifetime, or relationship.
   - Forbid unrecorded semantic inputs and post-hoc assignment of source IDs to an already-generated form.

2. **Persistent world and camera**
   - Give notes and field samples world-space depth rather than only a scalar display effect.
   - Keep material history in world or bounded depth-layer space and project it through the current camera.
   - Drive camera pose with a continuous path/velocity grammar independent of event-slot replacement.
   - Permit cue boundaries to change only forces/envelopes. Never replace or rebase world origin, seed, camera transform, or an existing feature identity at a cue boundary.
   - Trace stable world-feature IDs plus camera pose/velocity through every boundary. Predeclare maximum linear/angular velocity and acceleration; each integration step must remain inside `vMax*dt + 0.5*aMax*dt^2 + epsilon` (and the angular equivalent), with `epsilon = 1e-5` world units. Smooth pixels do not override a failed state trace.

3. **Only three visual materials**
   - witness light: a temporary readable note presence;
   - filament: a directional passage left by multiple contributors;
   - membrane/void: a thin boundary or absence created by accumulated field pressure.
   - No category palette, edge lines, synthetic satellites, independent fog, or decorative star background.

4. **Three overlapping clocks**
   - archive climate, encounter envelopes, and micro-life operate concurrently.
   - Remove the fixed global `ART_DURATION = 90` loop from this proof.
   - Begin at a deterministic already-running archive time and run for at least twelve minutes without resetting.

5. **Presentation**
   - No caption, hover label, or persistent controls.
   - In a later product seam, `life-weather` is a lifecycle state, not visible tab chrome: activity rail, header, sidebars, and tab bar are hidden over the existing BrowserWindow client area. It is not OS fullscreen or a second window.
   - `Escape` and a focus-revealed return control restore the exact previous workspace/focus. Pause and provenance are separate explicit review actions.
   - Provide a static reduced-motion frame and honest WebGL2 failure state.

## Technology boundary

- Host/model/score: TypeScript or the current module syntax, with a migration to TypeScript before product integration.
- First renderer: direct WebGL2/GLSL, no new package.
- WebGPU: use the completed capability receipt only as evidence that an A/B is possible. Implement a WebGPU backend only if this Living Archive Field design misses the pre-registered WebGL2 budget or if the same snapshot/seed/archive-time/score/canvas/DPR micro-spike demonstrates a necessary source-grounded material/pass advantage.
- Do not add Three.js, Rust/WASM, a native process, a database, audio, or a product IPC channel.

## Evidence set

- Before rendering, freeze one protocol containing: snapshot digest, seed, archive time, target canvas `1280 x 720` backing pixels at DPR `1.0`, capture times, randomized anonymous comparison order, observation questions, pass rules, owner/technical-verifier roles, and one no-retry acceptance round.
- Same snapshot / same seed reproducibility.
- Same snapshot / different archive time.
- Matched control with one preselected source phenomenon removed; record the predicted local region/cue and downstream propagation before seeing the output, while leaving every other input fixed.
- Current Gate 3B.5 and Living Archive Field captured at 0, 30, 90, 240, 480, and 720 seconds.
- Frame interval distribution, canvas/texture dimensions, WebGL errors, visibility pause/resume, and context-loss fallback on the current PC.
- Provenance receipt for the three largest visible transformations.
- Camera/world trace across every cue boundary, including origin, seed, stable feature IDs, camera pose/velocity, and integrator-bound result.

## Acceptance

1. In the pre-registered, randomized anonymous captures, the owner's first reading is not “white points on black” for the candidate; the condition names and receipts remain hidden until scoring is locked.
2. Without a legend and before reading telemetry, the owner can visually follow at least one energy passage into a later filament, boundary, or void.
3. Across cue replacement, the owner observes no camera cut or screen-space wake slip, and the technical verifier confirms that world origin/seed/feature identities were not replaced and every camera/coordinate step remained inside the predeclared integrator bound.
4. Depth is carried by parallax plus at least two of occlusion, focus/extinction, scale, or passage through material.
5. In twelve minutes there are at least three clearly different macro compositions, no complete blackout, no full-frame saturation, no fixed central attractor, and no short-cycle replay.
6. Before receipts are revealed, the owner distinguishes the pre-registered local difference and its later propagation in the one-source-removed control; telemetry alone cannot pass this gate. The receipt must then reproduce why without a random global reset.
7. The owner answers yes to all three: “my archive caused this,” “I want to keep watching for 90 seconds,” and “I want to revisit on another day.”
8. At the fixed `1280 x 720` / DPR `1.0` proof target, excluding the first five seconds and intentionally hidden intervals, twelve-minute frame intervals have `p95 <= 20 ms` and fewer than one percent exceed `50 ms`; no WebGL error or unrecovered context loss occurs.
9. Reduced-motion renders a deterministic composed still, and WebGL2 initialization/context failure shows an honest static failure state. At later product integration, `Escape`/return must restore the exact previous workspace and focus before that seam can pass.

The owner owns perceptual acceptance. The technical verifier owns receipt reproducibility, control isolation, state continuity, and performance. The acceptance round is run once; failed questions are not re-run with changed labels or cherry-picked captures.

## Stop conditions

- The result still reads as a star field, particle demo, graph, atom model, or decorative fog.
- Source removal is only visible in telemetry/receipt, not in the experience.
- Camera continuity requires event-specific patches instead of one world-space contract.
- Material beauty depends on untraceable presets or invented semantic relationships.
- WebGL2 misses the agreed frame budget after reducing resolution and sample count without altering the art claim; stop and run the same-score WebGPU A/B rather than optimizing blindly.
- Any owner question is answered no. Return to the representation or temporal grammar; do not add effects, audio, AI meaning, or product chrome.
